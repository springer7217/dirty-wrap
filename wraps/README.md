# Kids mud drawings — Model Y (2025+)

A Paint Shop wrap memorializing the finger drawings the kids left in the dried mud on the Juniper Model Y.

Built on the official `modely-2025-premium` UV template (1024x1024 PNG, under 1 MB). The rear badge in the photos reads Dual Motor, and the car has the 2025+ full-width light bar, so Premium is the matching template. Performance and Model Y L use different maps.

## What is on the car

Driver side (left islands on the template, lettering rotated so the tops face the center):

- WE ARE COOL, a smiley, and a ghost on the front door
- I AM BOO BOO BAKU!, Boo + Jesus, HI!, a flower, a star, and another smiley on the rear door

Passenger side (right islands, tops facing center):

- + Boobackooo, two ghost shapes, Jesus, wow, and a cross

Liftgate:

- HI HAVE A GOOD DAY!, a smiley, and a flower

The mud color is a dried gray-brown fill clipped to the paintable panels. Glass and the gaps between islands are left transparent, which Paint Shop ignores.

## Load it

File: `modely-2025-premium/kids_mud_drawings.png`

- App (v4.59.0+): Creations → Wrap → Upload
- Or copy the PNG into a `Wraps` folder on a USB drive (exFAT or FAT32, no map/firmware files)
- In the car: Toybox → Paint Shop → Wraps

Door art is unfolded outward on Tesla's template. If a line reads upside down in Paint Shop, mirror that side and re-export. This is a screen wrap only, not a vinyl cut file.
